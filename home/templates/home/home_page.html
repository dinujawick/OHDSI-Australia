Who are we?

Open science for better health decisions.

OHDSI Australia is part of the global Observational Health Data Sciences and Informatics community. We bring together people and organisations using the OMOP Common Data Model and open-source analytics to learn from observational health data.

Our work supports transparent, secure, and scalable research across a diverse health system, while respecting local governance and patient privacy.

Why OHDSI Australia?

Strengthening evidence generation, nationally.

Australia's health data is rich, but distributed across jurisdictions, institutions, and systems. OHDSI Australia helps connect local expertise, common standards, and shared analytical practice so participating organisations can produce trusted evidence at scale.

  • Support practical adoption of the OMOP Common Data Model
  • Connect Australian expertise with the global OHDSI network
  • Share tools, training, and implementation experience
  • Enable privacy-preserving federated research
